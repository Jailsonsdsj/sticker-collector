## Coins

Coins are used to buy albums and stickers.

**Earning**

- Coins are earned **only** by completing tasks.
- A task's reward defaults to its estimated effort in minutes. **One minute of effort = one coin.** The user may override the reward when creating the task.
- The effort estimate is a self-declared intention, not a measurement. A task estimated at 200 minutes and finished in 5 still pays 200 coins. The app does not police this.

**Spending**

1. Unlock an album.
2. Unlock a sticker directly, inside an unlocked album.
3. Buy a random sticker, inside an unlocked album.

**Returning**

- Selling a duplicate sticker returns **half the random-sticker price** of the album it came from. A duplicate is therefore always a net loss, under any values the user sets.

**Display rules**

- A locked album, its cover, and every locked sticker are displayed in black and white.
- An unlocked album, its cover, and every unlocked sticker are displayed in color.
