# Flow

The whole product is two loops sharing one currency.

**The earning loop.** You keep a to-do list — routines that recur on chosen weekdays, and one-offs with or without a due date. Completing anything pays coins equal to its effort in minutes. Coins accumulate in a single wallet.

**The spending loop.** You author albums from your own images: pick a cover, import stickers, sort them into four rarity tiers, and set the album's prices and odds. Sealing the album freezes those numbers. Then you spend coins — unlocking the album, then filling it, one sticker at a time or by gambling on random pulls. Completing an album unlocks a print-ready PDF: the reward for the whole cycle is a physical thing you can hold.

The tension between the loops is the point. Every coin spent on a sticker is a coin earned by doing something you meant to do. The collection is a record of effort, not of money.

```
        complete a task
              │
              ▼
        earn coins ──────────────┐
              │                   │
              ▼                   │
   unlock album · buy sticker     │  (self-set
   · pull at random               │   prices & rewards,
              │                   │   frozen at seal)
              ▼                   │
      complete the album          │
              │                   │
              ▼                   │
     export the print PDF ────────┘
```

Nothing in the app is bought with money. There is one player, one wallet, and one honest constraint: you cannot spend effort you did not spend.
